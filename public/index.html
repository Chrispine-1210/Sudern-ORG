03 / DELIVERY ARCHITECTURE FROM ENTRY TO OUTCOME

A measurable pathway
from learning to livelihoods.

Each transition should have clear eligibility rules, responsibility, documentation and feedback loops.

Assess finance readiness and support eligible opportunities.

05▤

Monitor results, protect participants and reinvest lessons.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Do not count training attendance as economic transformation.

Follow participants beyond the classroom. Record competencies, enterprise survival, access to customers, income changes and community benefit, using appropriate consent and verification.

05 / INSTITUTIONAL STEWARDSHIP TRUST BY DESIGN

Governance is not
a footer-page formality.

Good development work needs decision rights, independent oversight, financial discipline and transparent reporting from the outset.

⚖

Responsible governance

Proposed trustee oversight, conflict-of-interest controls, delegated authority and documented decisions.

  • Board and committee charters
  • Conflict declarations & approvals
  • Safeguarding & complaints pathways
▦

Financial accountability

Ring-fenced project budgets, dual authorisations, procurement evidence and periodic reporting.

  • Fund restrictions & approvals
  • Expenditure and variance records
  • Independent financial review
⌁

Monitoring, evaluation & learning

Outcome indicators, evidence standards, data protection and continuous improvement.

  • Baseline and follow-up measures
  • Verified evidence and data quality
  • Learning and corrective actions
